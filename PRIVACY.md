# Privacy Policy — AI Newsroom Studio

AI Newsroom Studio is a personal, local-only automation pipeline that
creates short news videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube
channel as unlisted videos.

## Data accessed
- YouTube account authorization (OAuth token) — used solely to upload
  the owner's own generated videos. The token is stored locally on the
  owner's machine (youtube_token.json) and is never transmitted
  anywhere else.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not collect, read, store, or share any other YouTube data
  (no watching history, no comments, no analytics, no channel data).
- We do not share any data with third parties.
- We do not sell or monetize any data.
- The app has no other users; it is run solely by its developer.
